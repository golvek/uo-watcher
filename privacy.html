Privacy Policy

UO Watcher does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device and never sent to any server:

  • Timer state (time remaining, pause status, task count, time bank, total work time, timer duration, widget position) is saved via localStorage and chrome.storage.sync.
  • Settings (overlay size, visibility toggles, colorblind mode, daily reset hour) are saved via chrome.storage.sync.
  • Cached stats are saved via chrome.storage.local to persist popup values between sessions.

This data is strictly functional -- required for the timer overlay and popup to operate. No data is collected for analytics, advertising, or any other purpose.

External Services

The extension popup may load an image from storage.ko-fi.com when displaying a Ko-fi support button. Ko-fi's privacy policy is available at ko-fi.com/home/privacy.

Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in the extension listing.
